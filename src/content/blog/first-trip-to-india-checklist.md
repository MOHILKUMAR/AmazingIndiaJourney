## Before you go

- Apply for your e-Visa at least 2–3 weeks ahead
- Check your passport is valid for 6 months
- Speak to your doctor about recommended vaccinations

## Money

Cards work in hotels and larger shops; carry small notes for tips and markets.

## What to wear

Light, breathable clothing that covers shoulders and knees is ideal for temples.

## Planning your Agra visit

Remember the Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays, so plan your Agra day for any other day of the week.
