Rajasthan is at its best from **October to March**, when days are warm and evenings are cool.

## October to March: peak season

Clear skies, pleasant temperatures and the big festivals: Diwali, the Pushkar Camel Fair and the Jaipur Literature Festival.

## April to June: hot season

Daytime temperatures often pass 40°C. If you travel now, plan sightseeing for early mornings.

## July to September: monsoon

Lakes in Udaipur fill up and the countryside turns green, a quieter, cheaper time to visit.
